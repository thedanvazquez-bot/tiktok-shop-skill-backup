# Hook Bank

8 hook styles. Pick 4 unique per bundle — no repeats within a single product's 4-prompt set.

| # | Style | When to use |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Curiosity | Product has a surprising or counterintuitive angle the viewer won't expect |
| 2 | Problem/Solution | Pain point is widely felt and the fix is visibly satisfying |
| 3 | Price Drop | Listing dropped recently or buyer is comparison-shopping on price |
| 4 | Discovery | Product is under-the-radar or new; viewer feels they found a gem |
| 5 | Comparison | A common alternative exists (old version, competitor, generic option) |
| 6 | Hidden Gem | Product is in a crowded category but the listing itself is overlooked |
| 7 | Gift Idea | Seasonal/holiday/occasion-driven purchase; recipient is identifiable |
| 8 | Seasonal | Calendar-anchored urgency (Q4, hurricane season, back to school, etc.) |

Pitfall: 4 prompts that read like 4 rewrites of the same hook style don't A/B/C/D test — they test the same concept against itself. Force diversity.

Fallback when only 2-3 styles feel right: pick the strongest 4 anyway, even if some feel forced. Test data beats aesthetic balance.
